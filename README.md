# 🏢 PWM Team Lookbook & Interactive Floor Maps

An interactive office directory and digital "Office Facebook" built for team members and visitors to explore workstation seating, team workflows, and colleague profiles across multiple floors.

---

### 🌐 Live Preview
Access the interactive floor map directly here:  
👉 **[https://chiarademeglio77.github.io/office_book/](https://chiarademeglio77.github.io/office_book/)**

*(Fully interactive in any modern desktop or mobile browser — no installations or login required.)*

---

### ✨ Features & Capabilities

- **Architectural Floor Plan Blueprint:** Accurate workstation pins mapped across Floors 34 and 35, highlighting occupied desks, colleague headshots, and open seating.
- **In-Depth Team Profiles:** Detailed cards showcasing roles, groups, office locations, and personal bios.
- **⚡ Desk Superpowers vs. ⚠ Real-World Kryptonites:** Humorous, authentic workplace strengths paired with day-to-day work habits (complete with custom badge stickers).
- **Dual Photo Showcase:** Side-by-side photography capturing real desk operations alongside parallel-life dream careers (from artisanal pastry chefs to wildlife sculptors and NASA commanders).
- **🔒 Interactive Throwback Archive:** A vintage 2016 intern lookbook snapshot hidden behind a dynamic blur spoiler overlay with a click-to-reveal toggle and enlarged modal zoom.
- **🎯 Passion & Hobby Filtering:** Interactive filter buttons (Pastry Baking, Sculpture, Astronomy, Italian Cuisine, Espresso, Origami) that dynamically dim non-matching workstations across the floor.
- **📝 Live Bio Editing:** In-browser toggle allowing team members to edit and preview bio descriptions on the fly.

---

### 🛠️ Built With

- **HTML5 & CSS3:** Semantic markup, CSS Grid, Flexbox, custom variables, and interactive transitions.
- **Vanilla JavaScript:** Zero external dependencies; lightweight client-side state handling for floor toggling, modals, and filtering.
- **GitHub Pages:** Instant, serverless cloud deployment.
