Section 0 · Introductions

👋 Hey Everyone — A Little About Me

"Hi everyone! I'm Elena, and I'm so excited to finally meet you all and settle into the floor. Growing up, I split my time between coastal Maine and Montreal—which basically means I have a lifelong loyalty to both rugged Atlantic coastlines and proper Montreal bagels, and I'm always happy switching between English and French."

"For college and grad school, I split my academic chapters between Georgetown and McGill, which kept that cross-border rhythm going strong. Before joining the team here, I spent my career at Oliver Wyman and Stripe—experiences that taught me to love untangling complex strategic problems and building clean, scalable operational systems."

"Outside of work, I'm almost always planning my next trip. I travel a lot—whether it's a spontaneous long-weekend flight with just a carry-on or exploring a new city's neighborhood cafés on foot. Please stop by my desk to say hello or swap travel stories!"

Section 2 · Interview / Q&A

🎙️ Rapid-Fire Icebreakers with Elena

Q1. What are your first impressions of the team and office vibe so far?

"Honestly, the warmth and energy on the floor stood out immediately. Coming from the worlds of consulting at Oliver Wyman and fast-paced fintech at Stripe, I love environments where people are sharp and rigorous but still take five minutes to chat over a morning espresso. Everyone has already been so generous with their time."

Q2. Since you travel so much, what is your #1 travel recommendation—and your non-negotiable airport habit?

"If you haven't done a late-autumn drive up the Maine coast followed by a train ride into Old Montreal, put it at the top of your list! As for airport habits: I am strictly team carry-on-only, no matter how many days the trip is, and my first stop in any new city is finding the best local bakery within walking distance."

Q3. How did studying at both Georgetown and McGill shape the way you collaborate?

"Georgetown gave me a deep appreciation for big-picture policy, global perspectives, and structured debate, while McGill grounded me in analytical rigor and a super international, bilingual community. Together, they taught me how to translate between very different stakeholders without losing the human story."

Q4. What are your go-to desk habits when you're in deep-focus mode?

"I'm an early-morning strategist—I do my best thinking with a black coffee, a clean legal pad for sketching out frameworks before touching a slide deck, and instrumental playlists in my headphones."
